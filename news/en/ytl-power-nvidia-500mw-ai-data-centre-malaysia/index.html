YTL Power and NVIDIA announce 500 MW AI data centre collaboration in Malaysia

Illustrative image
← Latest coverage

The companies said the planned AI infrastructure would be hosted at YTL's green data centre park in Kulai, Johor, which the December 2023 announcement described as a 500 MW facility.

YTL Power International and NVIDIA announced an AI infrastructure collaboration linked to the YTL Green Data Center Park in Kulai, Johor, on 8 December 2023.

What happened

The announcement connected NVIDIA's computing platform with YTL Power's development of the YTL Green Data Center Park. YTL said it planned to deploy NVIDIA H100 GPUs and NVIDIA AI Enterprise software, with the infrastructure intended to support AI computing services in Malaysia.

Key facts

  • Project: YTL Green Data Center Park
  • Location: Kulai, Johor, Malaysia
  • Sector: Data Center
  • Capacity stated in the 2023 announcement: 500 MW
  • Developer and owner: YTL Power International
  • Event category: Announcement

Why it matters

The collaboration was an early signal of the scale of power and digital infrastructure being planned for AI computing in southern Malaysia. This is analysis based on the verified project and event records; it does not indicate that the full campus had reached commercial operation at the time of the announcement.

Project and company context

YTL Power said the AI infrastructure would be hosted at the Kulai campus and owned and managed by its telecommunications subsidiary, YTL Communications. The 500 MW figure reflects the scale described in the December 2023 announcement and should not be treated as the project's current capacity without checking later project disclosures.
