Ningxia-Hunan 8 GW UHVDC link enters full operation

Illustrative image
← Latest coverage

The 1,616 km, +/-800 kV transmission project connects renewable-rich Ningxia with the load centre of Hunan.

The Ningxia-Hunan ultra-high-voltage direct-current project was placed into full operation on 20 August 2025, following a joint commissioning ceremony in Ningxia and Hunan.

What happened

Officials and State Grid representatives started the full transmission service after staged testing earlier in the summer. The link runs from the Zhongning converter station in Ningxia to the Hengyang converter station in Hunan and crosses Gansu, Shaanxi, Chongqing and Hubei.

Key facts

  • Project: Ningxia-Hunan +/-800 kV UHVDC Project
  • Route: Ningxia to Hunan, China
  • Sector: Grid & Transmission
  • Rated capacity: 8,000 MW
  • Voltage: +/-800 kV
  • Route length: 1,616 km
  • Developer and owner: State Grid Corporation of China
  • Event category: COD

Why it matters

The line creates a large eastbound transmission route for electricity from Ningxia's desert renewable-energy bases while adding supply capacity to Hunan. Its completion illustrates the role of long-distance UHVDC infrastructure in connecting resource regions with major demand centres.

Project and company context

The project completed a 168-hour trial and began initial 4,000 MW delivery on 29 June 2025. That milestone was partial service, not full COD; the 20 August event marked full commissioning at the project's 8,000 MW rated capacity.
