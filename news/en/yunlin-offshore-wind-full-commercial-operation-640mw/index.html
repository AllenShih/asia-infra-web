Yunlin Offshore Wind Farm enters full commercial operation at 640 MW

Illustrative image
← Latest coverage

All 80 turbines are grid-connected, and the project has moved into its operations and maintenance phase.

Yunneng Wind Power announced on 21 August 2025 that the 640 MW Yunlin Offshore Wind Farm had entered full commercial operation.

What happened

The project completed the licensing, regulatory and financing-agreement requirements needed for commercial operation. Its 80 wind turbine generators had achieved full grid connection in January 2025 before the later COD announcement.

Key facts

  • Project: Yunlin Offshore Wind Farm
  • Location: 8-17 km off Yunlin County, Taiwan
  • Sector: Offshore Wind
  • Generation capacity: 640 MW
  • Turbines: 80
  • Owner: Yunneng Wind Power Co., Ltd.
  • Event category: COD

Why it matters

Full operation adds one of Taiwan's largest offshore wind assets to the grid and moves the project from construction into long-term operations. The milestone also follows a separate full-grid-connection event, which should not be treated as identical to commercial operation.

Project and company context

Yunneng is a joint venture of Skyborn Renewables, TotalEnergies, EGCO Group and Sojitz. Electricity is supplied to Taiwan Power Company under two 20-year power purchase agreements, while TotalEnergies leads technical operations management.
