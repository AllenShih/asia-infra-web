# Microsoft announces US$1.7 billion cloud and AI investment in Indonesia

The four-year commitment covers new cloud and AI infrastructure, skills programmes and support for Indonesia's developer community.

Microsoft announced on 30 April 2024 that it would invest US$1.7 billion in Indonesia over four years, describing the commitment as the largest single investment in its 29-year history in the country.

## What happened

Chairman and CEO Satya Nadella announced the programme during Microsoft Build: AI Day in Jakarta. Microsoft said the investment would expand cloud and AI infrastructure, provide AI-skilling opportunities for 840,000 people and support the country's developer community.

## Key facts

- **Project:** Microsoft Indonesia Cloud & AI Infrastructure
- **Location:** Indonesia
- **Sector:** Data Center
- **Investment commitment:** US$1.7 billion over four years
- **Investor:** Microsoft
- **Event category:** Announcement

## Why it matters

The commitment signals continued hyperscale investment in Southeast Asia's largest economy and links physical cloud infrastructure with workforce and developer initiatives. This is analysis based on the announced programme; Microsoft did not allocate the full US$1.7 billion exclusively to a single data centre project.

## Project and company context

Microsoft said the investment builds on its 2021 plan to establish the company's first data centre region in Indonesia. The April 2024 amount covers a broader cloud and AI programme, so it should not be treated as disclosed project CAPEX for one facility.
