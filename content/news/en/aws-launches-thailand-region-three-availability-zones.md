# AWS launches Thailand cloud region with three Availability Zones

AWS said the new region operates from three Availability Zones and is backed by a planned investment of more than US$5 billion in Thailand.

Amazon Web Services launched the AWS Asia Pacific (Thailand) Region on 7 January 2025, allowing customers to run workloads and store data in the country.

## What happened

The launch added a Thailand-based AWS region composed of three Availability Zones. AWS said the zones are physically separate but connected through redundant, low-latency networks to support resilient cloud deployments.

## Key facts

- **Project:** AWS Asia Pacific (Thailand) Region
- **Location:** Thailand
- **Sector:** Data Center
- **Configuration:** Three Availability Zones
- **Long-term investment plan:** More than US$5 billion
- **Developer and operator:** Amazon Web Services
- **Event category:** COD

## Why it matters

The launch expanded in-country hyperscale cloud capacity and gave Thai organisations additional options for data residency and lower-latency services. The three-zone design also provides a foundation for customers that distribute workloads across separate facilities for resilience.

## Project and company context

AWS described the investment as a long-term commitment covering construction and operation of the region. The amount is not a disclosed CAPEX figure for a single data centre or Availability Zone.
